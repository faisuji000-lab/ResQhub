Offline Survival & First Aid Guide

Critical procedures to stay alive in disaster zones without internet.

💧 How to Make Flood Water Safe to Drink

1. Filter cloudy water through a clean cotton cloth or folded T-shirt into a container.
2. Boil vigorously for at least 1 full minute (3 minutes at higher altitudes).
3. If no fire is available: Add 2 drops of standard household unscented chlorine bleach per 1 Liter of clear water. Stir and wait 30 minutes before drinking.

⚡ Electrical Hazard & Submerged Wires

- NEVER step into water if power lines are submerged anywhere nearby.
- Turn off your home's main electrical breaker switch ONLY if you can do so while completely dry.
- If you feel tingling in your feet while wading in water: Stop immediately, hop away on one foot, or shuffle feet together without lifting them off the ground.

🐍 Flood Snakebite Emergency Action

1. Keep the victim calm and strictly still. Movement accelerates venom absorption.
2. Immobilize the bitten limb below heart level with a splint or firm cloth.
3. DO NOT cut the wound, DO NOT attempt to suck venom, and DO NOT apply ice or tight tourniquets.
4. Evacuate immediately to City Trauma Hospital (+91 98201 11223) for anti-venom vial administration.

🩹 Severe Wound & Bleeding Control

1. Apply firm, continuous direct pressure over the wound using a sterile cloth or clean clothing.
2. Elevate the injured limb above heart level if possible.
3. Do not remove the first cloth if blood seeps through; place a second cloth directly over it and continue firm pressure until medical help arrives.
